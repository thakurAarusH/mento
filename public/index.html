The problem 課題 · かだい

The most valuable knowledge is never written down.

  • Senior craftspeople retire and their know-how leaves with them
  • Training newcomers takes the best people away from their work
  • Manuals capture the steps, never the judgement behind them

How it works 仕組み · しくみ

Four simple steps from master to apprentice.

  1. 一 · 01

    Master demonstrates

    The master records a real task on a phone. No studio, no script.

  2. 二 · 02

    AI asks

    The AI finds the key moments and, using the manual as background, asks about what the manual does not explain.

  3. 三 · 03

    Master reviews

    Typed or spoken answers become knowledge drafts. Only what the master approves is ever shown to students.

  4. 四 · 04

    Student practices

    Students join with a code, study the lessons and practise with camera checks tied to the master's own clip.

Why MENTO 信頼 · しんらい

Built on trust, not guesswork.

Grounded answers only 根拠

Every tip a student sees comes from knowledge the master approved. If the evidence is unclear, MENTO says it cannot assess.

Human in the loop 人

Students' questions go straight to the master. The AI only drafts; nothing is approved automatically.

Local-first 地元

Runs on your own computer: vision AI, speech-to-text and read-aloud voices. Cloud AI is optional and always falls back to local.

Private by design 秘密

Consent on every device, personal master accounts and per-session invitations keep recordings inside your workshop.

Ready-made skills 技 · わざ

One platform. Any craft.

Each skill is a simple profile of tasks, tools, safety notes and a short manual. Four come ready to use; masters can describe their own craft in a minute.

Technical 技術 · ぎじゅつ

Under the hood.

One reusable engine. Skills are configuration profiles, not separate apps. Everything runs locally by default.

Private by design

  • Video, audio and knowledge stay on the master's computer. The AI (vision model, speech-to-text and read-aloud) runs locally, with no cloud AI.
  • The AI never decides why the master did something: it asks, and the master's own words are kept and labelled.
  • Students only ever see knowledge the master has approved.
